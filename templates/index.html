How It Works

Four Steps to Clear Hydrological Insights

From raw pixel ingestion to structured spatial analytics in milliseconds.

STEP 01

Upload an Image

Provide aerial, drone, or ground-level flood photography in JPG, PNG, or WEBP format.

STEP 02

AI Analyzes Features

The system evaluates multi-scale spectral, chromatic, and edge characteristics directly from image pixels.

STEP 03

Regions Segmented

Pixel-wise classification identifies flood-class regions and generates crisp shoreline contours.

STEP 04

Review & Export

Inspect coverage percentages, spatial distributions, and download high-resolution maps or JSON reports.

Which settings should I use?
• Fast Model (Default): Best for rapid sub-40ms edge screening.
• High-Precision Ensemble: Best for maximum boundary quality (18 features).
• Cluster Segmenter: For exploratory clustering without ground-truth masks.
Threshold (τ) Sensitivity:
• Lower (0.35–0.45): Muddy/shadowed water or inclusive detection.
• Default (0.50): Balanced for clear daylight scenes.
• Higher (0.55–0.65): Suppresses bright glare, wet asphalt, or conservative calls.

Understanding This Result

The flood coverage value represents the proportion of image pixels classified as flood by the model. It does not represent actual land area, water depth, or real-time emergency hazard status. Results depend on image quality, illumination, water turbidity, and specular surface reflections.

About FloodVision AI

Lightweight Environmental Image Intelligence

FloodVision AI develops lightweight classical machine learning models to identify flood-affected regions in aerial and terrestrial imagery. By combining multi-spectral color transformations with spatial feature engineering, it delivers efficient image segmentation without relying on computationally intensive neural networks.
